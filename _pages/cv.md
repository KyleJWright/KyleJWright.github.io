Mathematical physicist returning to research, aimed at the mathematical foundations of AI alignment. PhD and postdoctoral
work on symmetry and duality structures in string theory, quantum and conformal field theory: generalized geometry,
Courant and Lie algebroids, gauged sigma models. Industry experience in applied quantitative modelling under real-world
constraints, most recently supporting New Zealand's electrification. Applied gauge invariance to neural network
compression in a volunteer research collaboration, working directly with parameter-space degeneracy in large language
models.

## Research experience

**Iliad Intensive: Technical AI Alignment** <span style="float: right">Sep – Oct 2026</span><br>
London Initiative for Safe AI (LISA), London

- Completed a four-week full-time taught programme in foundational AI alignment research (learning theory,
  interpretability, agent foundations, computational mechanics), with a particular interest in the computational
  mechanics of world models: belief-state geometry and minimal predictive representations.

**AI Research Collaborator** <span style="float: right">Oct 2025 – Feb 2026</span><br>
Bradbury Group (volunteer research lab), remote

- Investigated symmetry-based compression of large language models: identified the gauge invariance structure of GPT-2's
  attention weights, derived gauge-invariant variables in an affine formulation, and implemented a Fisher-weighted SVD
  compression scheme in PyTorch that outperformed standard SVD at matched parameter budgets at the head level, where the
  gauge symmetry acts.

**Postdoctoral Researcher** <span style="float: right">Aug 2019 – Aug 2021</span><br>
New York University Abu Dhabi, UAE

- Applied group-theoretic and algebraic methods to classify symmetry structures in quantum field models, developing
  analytical frameworks to characterise their representations and physical constraints.

**Postdoctoral Researcher** <span style="float: right">Aug 2018 – Aug 2019</span><br>
Australian National University, Canberra

- Developed algebraic and geometric frameworks for symmetry and duality structures in string-theoretic sigma models;
  sole-authored publication unifying Lie algebroid gauging conditions via generalized geometry.
